Neurological and cardiac disorders are among the leading causes of death and disability worldwide, yet many remain challenging to diagnose early, monitor longitudinally, and treat effectively. The increasing availability of biomedical imaging, physiological signals, electronic health records, genomics, and other clinical data presents unprecedented opportunities for artificial intelligence (AI) to support the full continuum of care. However, translating AI advances into neurological and cardiac medicine requires more than predictive accuracy. Clinical AI systems must be multimodal, robust across institutions and populations, interpretable, uncertainty-aware, fair, and sufficiently trustworthy for use in high-stakes clinical environments.

TrustMedAI 2027 brings together researchers, clinicians, and practitioners working at the intersection of biomedical imaging, multimodal AI, and trustworthy AI for neurological and cardiac disorders. The workshop will explore AI methods across the full clinical pipeline—from diagnosis and early detection to treatment, intervention, longitudinal management, and post-treatment outcomes. We particularly encourage work that bridges methodological advances with clinically meaningful problems and that evaluates models not only by accuracy, but also by calibration, generalization, fairness, robustness, interpretability, and deployment readiness.
